Project Overview

About Smart Café

“Smart Café is a digital self-service café management system designed to make food ordering faster, easier, and more efficient.”

Traditional System vs. Smart Café System

How digitizing the café counter eliminates wait times and human calculation errors:

Workflow Parameter Traditional Café System ❌ Smart Café Kiosk System ✓
Menu Browsing Customer waits for waiter to bring a physical paper menu, which may be soiled or outdated. Instant digital interactive menu with real-time dish availability and instant category search.
Order Taking Waiter writes handwritten notes; potential handwriting misinterpretation. Direct customer selection with clear quantity selectors and custom preference options.
Bill Calculation Manual calculation by cashier; prone to arithmetic errors and manual discount oversights. 100% automated dynamic computation with exact 5% GST and conditional discount rules.
Invoice & Receipt Handwritten receipt or manual POS entry requiring significant queue time. Instant printable digital tax invoice with unique sequential Order ID (SC1001+) and barcode.
Kitchen Pipeline Verbal communication or paper slips lost in kitchen bustle. Live administrative order queue with multi-stage status progression tracking.
